# Choice Mobs & UI — art sources

This pack does not redraw anything. Every texture is a byte copy of a file already in `development_resource_packs/Choice Textures_rp/` on `main` (display name `v26.11.13 Choice Textures`, manifest `1.0.24`). Choice's ledger is the source of truth: `development_resource_packs/Choice Textures_rp/credits/SOURCE_TRACKING.md` and `CHOICE_PACK.md`.

Choice cannot tag every older PNG. These are the buckets that apply to the sheets this pack actually ships.

## Natural Texture Pack 1.0.89 (private)

Most animal entity sheets (farm animals, horses under `horse2`, cats, wolves, axolotl, bee, fox, frog, and the other animals recorded in the 2026-10-04 Choice entries) are **Natural Texture Pack 1.0.89**, Minecraft Marketplace, by Mojang.

That art is private. Do not publish this pack as a GitHub Release, and do not redistribute the Natural sheets. Chicken and sea turtle were later reverted off Natural back to the earlier Choice sheets (Choice issue #81). Those reverted sheets are what this pack copies.

## Faithful 64x Release 14

Some showcase hostiles and hole-fills in Choice are Faithful 64x r14 (warden, creaking, breeze, ender dragon, enderman, spider, and other fills recorded in `credits/entity_hole_fill.txt`). The unmodified Faithful license is `credits/faithful64/LICENSE.txt`.

https://faithfulpack.net/

https://faithfulpack.net/license

This pack does not include Faithful block or item placeholders. Spawn eggs were Faithful 64x r14 in Choice and are intentionally not in this pack.

## other-good (~32×)

Classic undead, illagers, and shulkers, plus some newer passives Choice kept at 32× (copper golem, happy ghast, nautilus), come from the "other good blocks to add" set recorded in `CHOICE_PACK.md`.

## Not included

Spawn eggs, blocks, and other item icons stay in Choice only. Player armor under `textures/models/armor/` (the commissioned adult and baby sheets) is not in this pack.
